Ticket #4471: dates parse mm/dd in eu locales

Analysis of 427 reports merged into ticket #4471 revealed two distinct problems for booking dates landing on the wrong day.

Problems Found

1. Date Parser Bug

Reports covered: 385

The date parser reads all dates as MM/DD globally, but tenants in day-first locales write dates as DD/MM. This flips the day and month. Includes 5 cases where dates shift by exactly one day (timezone or boundary edge cases).

Affected locales: en-GB, nl-NL, cs-CZ, it-IT, and others with day-first formatting.

GitHub issue: #7 Date parser reads month-first in day-first locales

2. Booking-v3 Form Hint Bug

Reports covered: 42

The booking-v3 form displays "MM/DD/YYYY" hint in day-first locales. Users follow the hint and type month-first, but the form parser expects day-first input, causing another date flip.

Affected locales: Same day-first locales using booking-v3 form.

GitHub issue: #8 Booking-v3 form hint confuses date input in day-first locales

Summary

Two distinct problems cause booking dates to land on the wrong day:

  • Parser bug: 385 reports from parser reading MM/DD in day-first locales
  • Form hint bug: 42 reports from booking-v3 form showing wrong hint in day-first locales

Total reports analyzed: 427 from April to October 2026.
