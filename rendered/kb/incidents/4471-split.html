How to tell them apart

  • parser: a day-first tenant, and the day and month in the report swap (7 April became 4 July). The report came by app, by email, or through a booking-v2 form.
  • hint: the same swap, but through the booking form of a booking-v3 tenant. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type the month first, and the form reads the tenant’s way. Fixing the parser does not fix this one; the form hint has to change.
  • shift: a few reports describe a one-day move, not a swap (“Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday”). The desk reads them as one-day moves (desk-notes.json) and counts them where their cause files them, under parser; their ticket is closed as a duplicate of the parser ticket.

Read the report body and the tenant, not the intake tag: intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and its tag disagrees with the cause on dozens of these reports.
